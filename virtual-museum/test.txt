TEST SUITE 1: 3D WORLD, NAVIGATION & SELECTION (PHASE 0)
================================================================================

Test 1.1: 3D Museum Navigation & First-Person Controls
------------------------------------------------------
* HOW TO INTERACT:
  1. Open http://localhost:3000 in your browser.
  2. Click on the 3D viewport to lock pointer/controls.
  3. Use W, A, S, D keys to walk around.
  4. Move the mouse to look around (360-degree rotation).
  5. Use Space / Shift (if applicable) or explore between gallery rooms.
* WHAT TO EXPECT:
  - Smooth 60 FPS rendering in Three.js/WebGL canvas.
  - Three themed galleries connected together:
    * Gallery 1: Smithsonian & Heritage Hall
    * Gallery 2: Smithsonian Technology & Science Hall
    * Gallery 3: Smithsonian Paleontology Hall
  - Collision boundaries prevent walking through outer walls.

Test 1.2: 15 3D Artifacts, Pedestals & Floating Labels
------------------------------------------------------
* HOW TO INTERACT:
  1. Walk up to pedestals in each gallery.
  2. Look at the yellow/gold brass nameplates on the pedestals.
* WHAT TO EXPECT:
  - 15 distinct 3D GLB artifacts are positioned across the 3 halls (e.g., Old Arrow Maker, Morse Telegraph, Apollo 11 Columbia, T-Rex Skull, Triceratops, Wright Flyer, etc.).
  - Pedestal nameplates display complete, readable artifact names without overlapping or text clipping.

Test 1.3: Selection Beacon & Active Focus
-----------------------------------------
* HOW TO INTERACT:
  1. Click directly on any 3D artifact (e.g., the T-Rex Skull or Apollo 11).
* WHAT TO EXPECT:
  - An animated Selection Beacon (pulsing ring / highlight) appears on the artifact.
  - The right-side AI Curator sidebar panel slides open.
  - The header displays the active artifact name and gallery tag.

================================================================================
TEST SUITE 2: CONTEXTUAL ARTIFACT Q&A — TYPE A (PHASE 1 & 4)
================================================================================

Test 2.1: The "What is this?" Baseline Contextual Test
------------------------------------------------------
* HOW TO INTERACT:
  1. Select the "Old Arrow Maker" statue (ART001) in Gallery 1.
  2. Open the AI Curator panel.
  3. Type and send: "What is this?"
* WHAT TO EXPECT:
  - The query is NOT refused.
  - The AI Curator identifies it as the "Old Arrow Maker" marble sculpture modeled in 1866 by Edmonia Lewis.
  - UI Source Tag: "Old Arrow Maker" | "Gallery 1: Smithsonian & Heritage Hall"
  - Confidence Badge: Shows confidence score.
  - Evidence Box: Expandable panel showing verified Smithsonian curated facts.

Test 2.2: Specific Attribute Inquiries on Selected Artifact
-----------------------------------------------------------
* HOW TO INTERACT:
  1. With "Old Arrow Maker" selected, ask:
     "What material is it made of?"
  2. Next, select the "Apollo 11 Command Module 'Columbia'" (ART006) in Gallery 2.
  3. Ask: "What mission was this used for and in what year?"
* WHAT TO EXPECT:
  - For Old Arrow Maker: Answers strictly that it is carved from marble.
  - For Apollo 11: Answers that it was the crew cabin for the historic July 1969 Apollo 11 first lunar landing mission.
  - Responses contain ZERO hallucinations and cite only curated catalog metadata.

================================================================================
TEST SUITE 3: CONFIDENCE GATE & OUT-OF-SCOPE REFUSAL (PHASE 2)
================================================================================

Test 3.1: Completely Irrelevant / Out-of-Scope Questions
--------------------------------------------------------
* HOW TO INTERACT:
  1. In the AI Curator panel, ask:
     "What is the weather in Tokyo today?"
  2. Next, ask:
     "What is 25 * 148?"
  3. Next, ask:
     "Who won the 2022 World Cup?"
* WHAT TO EXPECT:
  - The backend confidence score drops below the 0.50 threshold.
  - The system politely refuses with the standard message:
    "This information is not available in the curated museum knowledge base."
  - The LLM is NOT called (0 token waste, strict zero-hallucination guarantee).
  - The UI clearly indicates the refusal with appropriate confidence telemetry.

Test 3.2: General Semantic Museum Questions (Type B Query)
----------------------------------------------------------
* HOW TO INTERACT:
  1. Deselect any active artifact (or ask without selecting).
  2. Ask: "Tell me about the giant meat-eating dinosaur skull from the Cretaceous period."
* WHAT TO EXPECT:
  - Sentence Transformer embeddings perform cosine retrieval.
  - Automatically matches and retrieves "Tyrannosaurus rex Skull" (ART012).
  - Cosine similarity > 0.75 (above 0.50 threshold).
  - Returns a rich, grounded description of the T-Rex skull and its discovery in Montana.

================================================================================
TEST SUITE 4: PERSONALIZATION, MEMORY & TONES (PHASE 3)
================================================================================

Test 4.1: Conversation Memory & Pronoun Resolution
---------------------------------------------------
* HOW TO INTERACT:
  1. Select "Tyrannosaurus rex Skull" (ART012).
  2. Ask Turn 1: "What is this artifact?"
     -> Expect: Description of the T-Rex Skull.
  3. Ask Turn 2: "When was it discovered?"
     -> Expect: Understands "it" refers to the T-Rex skull and answers "1902".
  4. Ask Turn 3: "Who discovered it?"
     -> Expect: Answers "Barnum Brown".
* WHAT TO EXPECT:
  - Multi-turn conversation retains context across all turns in the session.
  - Sliding memory window tracks last 3 exchanges seamlessly.

Test 4.2: Curator Tone Adaptation
---------------------------------
* HOW TO INTERACT:
  1. Select "Wright Flyer" (ART008).
  2. Select Tone: "Educational" (from tone dropdown/buttons).
     Ask: "Explain its significance."
     -> Expect: In-depth scholarly response with aeronautical history and design context.
  3. Change Tone to: "Concise".
     Ask: "Explain its significance."
     -> Expect: Brief 2-3 sentence summary highlighting key facts only.
  4. Change Tone to: "Friendly".
     Ask: "Explain its significance."
     -> Expect: Warm, welcoming, conversational museum guide tone.
* WHAT TO EXPECT:
  - The factual content remains 100% identical and grounded; only the formatting style adapts.

Test 4.3: "You Might Also Like" Recommendation Engine
------------------------------------------------------
* HOW TO INTERACT:
  1. Ask any question about an artifact (e.g. ART001 "Old Arrow Maker").
  2. Look at the bottom of the AI Curator response.
* WHAT TO EXPECT:
  - 3 related artifact suggestion cards are displayed (computed via 384-d vector embeddings).
  - Excludes the current artifact and previously viewed artifacts.
  - Clicking on a suggested artifact navigates/highlights that artifact in the museum!
