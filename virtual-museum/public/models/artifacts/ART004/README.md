## Description

The Page Induction Coil is an electromagnetic device developed by
Charles G. Page around 1868. The object is recorded by the National
Museum of American History as a patent model.

The artifact is associated with the historical development of
electromagnetic devices and electrical technology. It provides a
physical representation of an invention from the nineteenth-century
development of electrical engineering.

The object is particularly relevant to the study of the development of
electricity, electromagnetism, and electrical devices during the
nineteenth century.

---

## Historical Significance

The Page Induction Coil is an example of a nineteenth-century patent
model associated with the development of electromagnetic technology.

Its inclusion in the National Museum of American History's
"Work and Industry: Electricity" collection and the exhibition
"Lighting a Revolution" makes it useful for understanding the history
of electrical technology.

The artifact can be used in the virtual museum to demonstrate how
historical electrical and electromagnetic devices contributed to the
development of modern electrical engineering.

- The model is suitable for real-time rendering.
- Meter-based scale is useful for AR placement.
