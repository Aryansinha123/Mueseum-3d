## 2. Historical Significance

Pioneer 10 was the first spacecraft to navigate through the Asteroid Belt, conduct a close-up flyby of Jupiter (December 1973), and achieve escape velocity from our Solar System.

Launched on **March 2, 1972** aboard an Atlas-Centaur rocket from Cape Canaveral, Pioneer 10 revolutionized planetary science by returning groundbreaking high-resolution images and radiation readings of Jupiter and its moons.

It also carries the iconic **Pioneer Plaque**, a gold-anodized aluminum plate featuring visual messages intended for potential extraterrestrial intelligence.

## 4. Spacecraft Design & Equipment

- **High-Gain Antenna:** 2.74-meter (9 ft) parabolic dish reflector
- **Power System:** Four Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generators (RTGs)
- **Scientific Instruments:** Magnetometer, Charged Particle Instrument, Cosmic Ray Telescope, Geiger Tube Telescope, Imaging Photopolarimeter
- **Interstellar Message:** Pioneer Plaque engraved with human figures, solar system map, and hydrogen hyperfine transition key.
