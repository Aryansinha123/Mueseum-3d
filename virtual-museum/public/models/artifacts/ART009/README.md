## 2. Historical Significance

The Mercury Pressure Suit was worn by astronaut **Alan B. Shepard Jr.** during the first manned spaceflight launched by the United States on **May 5, 1961**.

Shepard's sub-orbital flight was launched from **Pad 5 at Cape Canaveral** using a **Redstone rocket**. Approximately 15 minutes later, Shepard splashed down in the Atlantic Ocean.

The suit represents an important artifact from the early period of American human spaceflight and the **Project Mercury** program.

---

## 3. Suit Development

The Mercury spacesuit was a:

- Close-fitting suit
- Two-layer suit
- Full-pressure suit

It was developed by the **B.F. Goodrich Company** from their **Mark IV pressure suit**, which had previously been used by the U.S. Navy.

NASA selected the suit in **1959** for use during Project Mercury.

During the Mercury program, the suit underwent minor modifications, primarily involving the shoulders.
