## Rights and Usage

The Smithsonian record identifies the work as **CC0 / Public Domain**. The Smithsonian states that the media is in the public domain and may be copied, modified, and distributed without contacting the Smithsonian. It also recommends attributing the Smithsonian, contributing modifications or improvements back, and not misrepresenting the dataset or its source.

The asset is being used in this project for educational and academic purposes as part of a virtual museum and AR demonstration.
